Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which the GrowFin (the "App," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, maintains, and discloses information collected from users (each, a "User") of the GrowFin mobile application. This policy applies to the App and all products and services offered by GrowFin. GrowFin is a personal finance app designed to help users track spending and saving.

1. Information We Collect and Store

To provide the core functionalities of saving more and tracking spending, the GrowFin App necessarily collects and securely stores user-inputted and generated data. This data is linked to a unique user identifier assigned upon app usage or account creation.

  • User Account Data: When you use the App, a unique identifier is assigned to you to store your data securely. If we introduce optional registration, we may collect email or other identifying information you choose to provide.
  • Financial and Usage Data: We collect and store all financial data you actively input into the App, including transaction records (amounts, dates, categories), saving goals, budgets, and other financial metrics. This data is essential for providing the App's tracking and analysis services.
  • App Permissions: The App may request access to device features (e.g., storage for backups) to enhance functionality. We only access the data strictly necessary for the service you requested.

All collected financial data is stored securely and is used exclusively for the purpose of running the GrowFin application and providing you with personal finance tracking services.

2. Log Data and Aggregate Data

In the event of an error or application crash, the App may use third-party products (e.g., Google Play Console) to collect data and information (referred to as "Log Data"). This Log Data may include information such as your device's Internet Protocol ("IP") address, device name, operating system version, the configuration of the app when utilizing our Service, the time and date of your use of the Service, and other statistics. This information is used solely for analyzing crashes, monitoring aggregate usage patterns, and improving the App.

3. Cookies

The GrowFin App does not use "cookies" (small data files stored on your device) explicitly. However, the App may use third-party code and libraries that use "session cookies" or similar mechanisms to collect information and improve their services. You have the option to either accept or refuse these and know when a cookie is being sent to your device.

4. Security

We value your trust in providing us with your personal and financial information, and we strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. But remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

5. Children’s Privacy (Ages 13 and Under)

Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we will be able to do necessary actions.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
